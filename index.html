Frequently asked questions

Is my slot confirmed right after I submit the form?

No. Submitting sends us a request. Your slot is confirmed once we verify your payment, and our team messages you on WhatsApp.

Which time zone are the appointments in?

All appointment times are Bangladesh Standard Time (UTC+6). If you are abroad, convert from BST when choosing.

What's the difference between Batch and Individual?

Batch is a 1.5-hour small-group session on Sunday nights (5:00 PM or 6:30 PM BST) where several students build their CVs together. Individual is a 1-hour session, any day within booking hours, focused only on your CV and goals.

What should I prepare?

Your current CV (if any), academic results, publications, experience, achievements and a professional photo. Having a free Overleaf account helps.

How do I join the session?

After confirmation, you'll receive a Google Meet link. Join from a laptop if possible so you can edit along.

Can I reschedule?

Message us on WhatsApp with your Booking ID before the session and we'll try to find another slot.
